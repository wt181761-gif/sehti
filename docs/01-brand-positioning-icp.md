# Brand Positioning And ICP

## Brand Lock

Arabic brand name: `صحتي`

English name below it: `SEHTI`

Domain: `sehti.online`

Brand meaning: `My Health`. The name feels personal, simple, Moroccan, and easy to say in ads, calls, delivery confirmation, and UGC.

Recommended tagline:

```text
صحتي، طبيعية
```

Alternative tagline for hero sections:

```text
حلول طبيعية لمشاكل حقيقية
```

## Core Positioning

`صحتي` is a Moroccan DTC natural health brand that sells focused natural formulas for daily problems Moroccan men and women already suffer from: sensitive teeth, bloating, and joint/back pain.

The store must not feel like dropshipping. It must feel like the brand owns the products, controls the formulas, understands Moroccan bodies, and has proof behind every ingredient.

## One-Sentence Positioning

```text
صحتي هي علامة مغربية للصحة الطبيعية، تجمع بين مكونات معروفة في ثقافتنا ودعم علمي واضح، لتقديم حلول بسيطة لمشاكل يومية تؤثر على الثقة، الراحة، والحياة اليومية.
```

## Why Customers Should Pay More

We are not selling a cheap generic product. We sell:

- A Moroccan brand identity customers can trust.
- A clear formula for one specific problem.
- Ingredient education in Arabic/Darija.
- Social proof from Moroccan-looking customers.
- COD with no financial risk.
- Clean premium packaging and a serious brand website.
- A guarantee and transparent policies.

## Trust Psychology In Morocco

Moroccan customers trust through layers:

- Familiarity: Arabic/Darija language, Moroccan names, Moroccan cities, Moroccan use cases.
- Authority: ingredients explained with science and tradition.
- Social proof: reviews from people like them, with cities, ages, and specific results.
- Low risk: COD, free delivery, simple return policy.
- Professionalism: polished website, consistent branding, clear policies, responsive support.
- Cultural resonance: miswak, turmeric, laban/olibanum, herbs, family health.

- A capital `S` inside a circle.

صحتي

## ICP 1: Sensitive Teeth And Smile Confidence

Product:

```text
مسحوق المسواك الطبيعي ضد حساسية الأسنان
```

Main buyer:

- Men and women, 22-55.
- Students, employees, shop owners, social workers, brides/grooms, anyone embarrassed by teeth.
- Uses Facebook, TikTok, Instagram.
- Buys for confidence, not only dental health.

Core pain:

```text
كنشرب القهوة أو الماء البارد وكيضربني الألم. كنضحك ونغطي فمي بيدي. كنخاف تكون ريحة فمي باينة للناس.
```

Core desire:

- Smile without hiding.
- Drink hot/cold without pain.
- Feel clean when speaking near people.
- Use something natural and culturally trusted.

Trust proof:

- Miswak heritage.
- WHO-style authority mention.
- Clove/eugenol explanation.
- Customer before/after confidence stories, not medical cure claims.

## ICP 2: Bloating And Digestive Discomfort

Product:

```text
خليط الأعشاب الهاضمة ضد الانتفاخ
```

Main buyer:

- Mostly women, 25-50, but men also buy.
- Office workers, teachers, moms, women with social events.
- Uses TikTok/Instagram for relatable problem videos.

Core pain:

```text
بعد الماكلة كرشي كتنفخ، كنحس بالثقل والحريق، وكنبغي غير نحل الحزام أو نبدل الملابس.
```

Core desire:

- Feel light after meals.
- Wear clothes comfortably.
- Stop worrying after every meal.
- Find a simple daily habit instead of complicated diets.

Trust proof:

- Mint, fennel, anise tradition in Moroccan homes.
- Digestive science explained simply.
- Reviews from Moroccan women with cities.

## ICP 3: Joint And Back Pain

Product:

```text
مزيج الكركم واللبان ضد آلام المفاصل
```

Main buyer:

- Men and women, 38-70.
- Manual workers, drivers, parents, older customers.
- Often purchased by daughter/wife/son for parent.
- Facebook is strongest.

Core pain:

```text
كنفيق مع الصباح وظهري مشدود. كنوقف من الصلاة بصعوبة. الحبوب كتعاون شوية ولكن كنخاف على المعدة والكلاوي.
```

Core desire:

- Move with less discomfort.
- Pray, walk, work, and sleep easier.
- Use natural support instead of relying only on pills.
- Feel independent again.

Trust proof:

- Turmeric and black pepper absorption.
- Boswellia/olibanum tradition.
- Black seed cultural and Islamic trust.
- Testimonials from older Moroccan customers.

## Brand Voice

Use Moroccan Arabic with Modern Standard Arabic structure. It should feel warm, serious, and credible.

Do:

- Speak directly to the problem.
- Explain the ingredient logic.
- Use proof without sounding like a fake medical ad.
- Use `الدفع عند الاستلام`, `توصيل مجاني`, `ضمان الرضا`.
- Mention customer cities: Casablanca, Rabat, Fes, Agadir, Marrakech, Tangier.

Do not:

- Sound like cheap dropshipping.
- Use exaggerated cure claims.
- Use weight loss, sexual, miracle, or disease cure language.
- Overuse countdown scarcity outside the checkout upsell.
